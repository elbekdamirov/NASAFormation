# NASAFormation asset credits

The terrain, mineral deposits, equipment, machines, suit, survey probe, interface elements, and effects are original assets generated by the game source. The local thumbnail atlas renders the same 3D models used by the game. The NASAFormation logo comes from the project owner’s supplied artwork. No network connection is required.

The ambient and effects WAV files are synthesized for the game. Footsteps, mineral-specific mining contact and fracture cues, machine loops, the breathing warning, and tool clicks are synthesized in `game/nur_audio.gd`. The original Mission Control script was recorded with the Microsoft Hazel Windows speech voice and is bundled as offline audio with matching captions.

The star field uses the galactic-coordinate edition of [Deep Star Maps 2020](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851), NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio, Ernie Wright, and ESA/Gaia/DPAC for Gaia DR2. The game adjusts its color and visibility for time of day and weather. The soundtrack contains original ambient and percussion stems generated with `Generate-Music.mjs` and encoded as Vorbis.

The bundled Godot engine and its third-party libraries are covered by `runtime/Godot-LICENSE.txt` and `runtime/Godot-COPYRIGHT.txt`.
Curiosity's [Murray Buttes imagery](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/farewell-to-murray-buttes-image-4/) and [wind-carved cliff panorama](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/nasas-curiosity-captures-dawn-breaking-on-wind-carved-cliffs/) by NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS informed the procedural sediment layers and rock wear. The map and resource placements are fictional.
The orbital atlas bundles two tiles from the [NASA/JPL Mars Trek WMTS API](https://trek.nasa.gov/tiles/apidoc/trekAPI.html?body=mars). The real elevation color hillshade uses Mars Global Surveyor MOLA and Mars Express HRSC data. The landing sector is a separate simulated map. The sky shader samples the blue twilight halo from [Curiosity Mastcam PIA19401](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/sunset-at-mars-gale-crater-curiosity/), NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS, obtained through the NASA Image and Video Library API. Both assets are archived and work offline.
The Earth arrival uses NASA Johnson Space Center's [Apollo 17 Blue Marble photograph AS17-148-22727](https://images.nasa.gov/details/as17-148-22727) as an illustration of the simulated arrival.

Final upgrade art: app-mark.svg and uzbekistan-patch.svg are original local vector assets. Procedural astronaut portrait, ore variants, relief shaders, archive props and decoration models are authored in the bundled GDScript. Generated footsteps and rain contain no sampled commercial soundtrack.

Restored identity: the creator-supplied game/nasaformation.png is reused for the menu, runtime window icon and regenerated 32/64/256 Windows launcher icon. No new logo artwork was substituted. Sky cloud fields are original procedural shaders.
